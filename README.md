Overview

RentEase is a web-based furniture and appliance rental platform designed to provide customers with an affordable and flexible alternative to purchasing household items. Users can browse products, choose rental plans, schedule deliveries, manage rentals, and request maintenance services through a simple and user-friendly interface.
